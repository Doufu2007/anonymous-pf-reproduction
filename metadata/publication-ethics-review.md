# Anonymous code/data publication review

Scope: original audit implementations, synthetic outputs and public bibliographic metadata.

- Identity: release-file scan checks author identifiers, correspondence, account links, local paths and credentials. Git account/history anonymity is a separate limit; the repository account is not an anonymous hosting service.
- Data: simulated states and observations; no participant, patient, animal or proprietary data are distributed.
- License: MIT for code (`LICENSE-CODE`); CC BY 4.0 for generated outputs (`LICENSE-DATA`). Public metadata retains its providers' terms.
- Redistribution: no third-party article PDF/HTML, extracted full text, source passage collection or manuscript file is included. Source locators and original hashes are retained.
- Evidence: historical semantic-review decisions retain original hashes and do not clear the updated manuscript. Likelihood caps, operator costs and profiling are separate measurements.
- Boundaries: static checks and fixed-seed replay do not establish independent or cross-hardware replication, source-algorithm fidelity or journal acceptance.
- Approval: the author requested this update. This records bounded publication checks, not institutional ethics certification or final manuscript approval.
